Pre-Class Video Guide

Watch the fundamentals before class — spend class time on what videos can't give you.

01How This Course Runs

This course runs in a flipped format. For most weeks, we assign publicly available lectures from CMU's database courses — 15-445/645 Intro to Database Systems (Prof. Andy Pavlo, Fall 2025) and 15-721 Advanced Database Systems (Spring 2024) — as pre-class material. These are among the best database systems lectures in the world, and they are free.

Class time is then spent on what videos cannot give you:

  • Tuesday — a condensed recap of the week's core ideas, the instructor's perspective on where the field is heading, and discussion of the recommended papers.
  • Thursday — deep dives, hands-on sessions, and project office hours with direct feedback on your team's work.
Watch before Tuesday's class. Videos marked Core are assumed knowledge for that week's discussion and are in scope for the two quizzes, together with in-class material. Optional videos are for going deeper and are not quiz material. Weeks without videos are covered entirely in class.

Full playlists: 15-445 Fall 2025 · 15-721 Spring 2024. All videos © CMU Database Group, used as publicly shared course material.

03Notes

  • Grading is unchanged. Everything in the syllabus — Project 60% / Review 10% / Quiz 30% — stays exactly as announced. This guide only describes how lecture content is delivered.
  • Quiz scope. Each quiz covers in-class material plus the Core videos assigned up to that point.
  • Videos are graduate-friendly but not graduate-level everywhere. 15-445 lectures build the foundation; in-class sessions push into the research depth expected of a graduate course. If a video feels easy, come to class with questions about what it left out.
  • Mismatch weeks (10, 11, 15) have no suitable public lecture — these are covered entirely in class and lean on the recommended papers.
