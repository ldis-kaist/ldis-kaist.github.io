## Motivation

LLM inference and serving engines (vLLM and friends) differ widely in mechanisms and performance, and no unified model explains their behavior across hardware and objectives.

## Goal

A unified performance model of LLM inference engines: measure and break down throughput/latency across hardware settings, model multiple engines in one framework, and identify bottlenecks and preconditions to harness future hardware.
