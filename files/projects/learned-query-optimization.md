## Motivation

LLMs and learned models promise better query optimization (including cardinality estimation), but current approaches need heavy training and offer little robustness.

## Goal

Benchmark LLM-based query optimization, then develop better methods using multi-path reasoning and self-reflection — minimizing training overhead, reliance on external models, and optimization latency.
